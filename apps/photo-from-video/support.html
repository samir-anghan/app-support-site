Common questions

The app can't see my videos or can't save a photo

Photo From Video needs access to your photo library. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Photo From Video and choose Full Access.

A video takes a long time to open

If the video is stored in iCloud Photos, it has to download to your device first. Check that you're connected to the internet and try again.

I bought the unlock but the app still shows the free limit

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
