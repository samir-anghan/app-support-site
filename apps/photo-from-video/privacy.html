Photo From Video Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Developer: Sam Anghan

Photo From Video lets you pick a video from your photo library, find one exact frame and save it as a photo. All of this happens on your device. The app doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data.

What the app accesses

  • Your photo library. With your permission, the app opens the video you choose and saves the frame you pick back to your library. It only reads the videos you select, and it doesn't copy them into app storage.
  • Videos stored in iCloud. If a video you pick is stored in iCloud Photos, your device's Photos system may download it from Apple so the app can open it. This works the same way as viewing the video in the Photos app.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. It sends no data to the developer or to any third party.

Processing on your device

Frames are extracted on your device. Your videos and photos are never uploaded.

Stored on your device

The app keeps a count of how many free exports you've used. This count stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer doesn't receive your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with the App Store whether the unlock has been purchased.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the iOS share sheet sends the photo to the app or contact you pick. That transfer is controlled by you and by the app you share to.

Deleting your data

Photos you save stay in your photo library, where you can delete them at any time. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. You can also turn off photo access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Contacting support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you include in your message. This information is used only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
